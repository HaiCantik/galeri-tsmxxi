# Privacy Policy - Media Studio

Last updated: November 2026

Media Studio is a personal web app for uploading and editing photos and videos.

## Data we access
The app requests the Google Drive scope `drive.file`. It can only access files
and folders that the app itself created (the "MediaStudio" folder). It cannot
see or modify any other file in your Google Drive.

## How data is used
Photos and videos you upload are stored only in that Google Drive folder and are
displayed back to you inside the app. We do not sell, share, or use your data
for advertising, and we do not store it on any other third-party service.

## Data retention and deletion
Files remain in the Google Drive folder until deleted from the app or directly
from Google Drive. You can revoke the app's access at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## Contact
For questions, contact the app owner via the email listed on the app's
Google OAuth consent screen.
